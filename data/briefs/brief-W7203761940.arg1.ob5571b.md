## Paper direction
A paper here would argue that the argument from worldly randomness requires an explicit aggregate-level evidential premise, rather than relying on the unsupported claim that historical outcomes are often morally indiscriminate and non-monotonic. The strongest reply to anticipate is that neither worldview predicts reliable earthly desert and that local patterns therefore cannot decide between them.
